Calm AI Theme

A cook thinks about the dish through the hands. Touch, sound, smell, and sight keep the cook in a multisensory, analog loop with the fish, which helps them focus while much else happens ambiently in the background, and what the hands notice feeds their thinking about flavor, texture, and style. Current AI adds ability but pulls people out of that loop, by competing for attention or taking over the task. Calm AI keeps the loop, and can make it richer.

Directions

Each subtheme is a direction on the plane. All three are done by blending AI into the environment and the task.

1

Enhanced analog engagement

Make the multisensory loop between the person and the material richer.

2

Ambient awareness

Sense more of what is going on around the activity, ambiently, so attention stays free to move and the hands stay in the loop.

3

Extended physical ability

Do more in physical space: AI, robots, and devices extend what hands and bodies can do.

Ways

More than one route leads up and right. The knife above is one of them.
