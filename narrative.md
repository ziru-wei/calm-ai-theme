# Calm AI: Vision Writeup

<p class="by">Ziru Wei</p>

## Motivation: tools that cut the loop

People do skilled physical work through a loop: they touch the material, sense how it responds, and adjust. A knife in the hand is not only a means to cut, it is how a cook feels the food. We argue that two kinds of tools break this loop, and that a third kind is possible.

The first kind is the clumsy tool. It is heavy and dull, and it cuts the loop from the tool side: the person can barely reach the goal, and the effort of operating the tool pushes the material out of attention. Both ability and engagement stay low.

The second kind is current AI. It raises what a person can reach, but it does so by competing for attention or taking over the task. The person is pulled out of the material and into a conversation with the system, so engagement drops even as ability rises. The loop is cut from the AI side.

## Framing: ability and engagement

We place any tool on a plane with two axes. Ability is how much a person can reach with the tool. Engagement is how much the person stays in touch with the material. Four cases fall out, and only one is high on both.

## Calm AI: raising ability while keeping the loop

Calm AI is AI that raises what a person can do while attention stays free to move. The person stays in touch with the material through the same multisensory, analog loop, and the AI can make that loop richer instead of replacing it.

Two principles follow. The AI does not disturb: it never competes with the material for attention. It does not take over: the person remains the one who acts. These are constraints on any design, not subthemes of their own.

We use engagement in a specific sense: staying in touch with the material. It is not time on task or enjoyment. A system can be used for hours and still score low, if the person only talks to it.

## Ways: what moves a system up and right

Ways say how the AI is built into the activity. The directions that follow say where on the plane a project lands. All three ways blend AI into the environment and the task, and each improves a different thing: the tool, the spatial interface, or the proactive agent.

Proactivity is a way, not a direction. It is one mechanism, next to the environment, for reaching the same quadrant.

## Three directions

Each direction leaves a baseline and ends in the Calm AI quadrant, but they differ in what the AI adds and where on the plane they land. All three work by blending AI into the environment and the task.

<div class="slot dp" id="slot-dirs"></div>

Reading the charts: in direction 1 the arrows start from Current AI and rise, so reach is kept and engagement ends high but not far to the right. In direction 2 they can start from any of the three baselines, and ability means awareness ability. In direction 3 they end far to the right with engagement kept moderately high, and ability means action ability.

## Concrete projects on the triangle

A list of three directions cannot hold a project that belongs to two of them. We therefore arrange the directions as the corners of a triangle. A project is a shape with a start and an end, each with its own size, so it can sit on one corner, run along an edge, or cross the inside.
